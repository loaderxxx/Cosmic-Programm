## 3. Human research requests

The owner is willing to consider legitimate research, collaboration, licensing and technical due-diligence requests.

Before systematic study or reuse of Protected Material, the requesting party should contact the owner through the repository owner's available private contact channel and describe:

1. identity and organization;
2. purpose of access;
3. material requested;
4. whether AI/ML systems will process it;
5. retention period and storage location;
6. whether results will be published or commercialized.

Do not place confidential details in public issues or pull requests.

## 4. Public repository limitation

For repositories marked public, GitHub necessarily makes content accessible through GitHub's service and public repositories may be viewed or forked as permitted by GitHub's terms.

This notice does not claim that a public repository can technically prevent copying or automated collection. It records the owner's rights reservation, use restrictions, and permission boundary.

Protected IP that must not be exposed to third-party model providers or uncontrolled automated analysis should remain in a private repository / private core.

Absence of a separate licence file, a permissive statement, or an explicit prohibition does not create an implied licence to use Protected Material beyond rights that are independently granted by applicable law or platform terms.

## 7. Permission record

Authorizations must be explicit, scoped and recorded before the relevant use.

Minimum record:

- requester;
- date;
- material;
- permitted use;
- prohibited uses;
- duration;
- territory, if relevant;
- confidentiality terms;
- attribution requirements, if any;
- owner approval evidence.
