## What we do

COSMOSYNTH is an independent research programme connecting **space agencies, companies, technologies, capabilities and industry relationships** to evidence-based mission concepts and testable system architectures.

We do not study a space programme in isolation. We examine who provides each capability, where organizations complement or compete with one another, which interfaces remain uncertain, and what evidence would justify the next engineering step.

Our current output is an inspectable research foundation: organization dossiers, capability analysis, source-linked industry connections, comparative studies and proposed experiments. **We do not present these studies as operational space systems, supplier certifications or existing partnerships.**

## From research to a testable system

**A mission question → required capabilities → candidate organizations → interface checks → a bounded experiment → evidence.**

For example, a ground-contact study can connect spacecraft operations, station scheduling and failure recovery. The useful output is not a list of logos: it is a clear allocation of responsibilities, a comparison of alternatives, the unresolved interfaces and a reproducible test proposal. [Inspect the system options](ATLAS/SYSTEM_OPTIONS/README.md).

Our own [Mission A/B/C concepts and pilot entry](PILOTS/README.md) connect this research to autonomy and operations, lunar resource intelligence and modular infrastructure. They remain concepts, not completed demonstrations.

## For agencies, companies and research teams

**Agencies:** inspect our evidence, ecosystem map and proposed contribution before discussing a programme route.

**Companies:** review how your publicly described capabilities are represented; provide corrections or a non-confidential interface question.

**Universities and engineering teams:** challenge an assumption, suggest a baseline or help scope an independently reviewable experiment.
