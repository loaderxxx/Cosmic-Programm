**A useful relationship map distinguishes evidence from possibility.** Our source study records several kinds of connection; they must not be displayed as equivalent lines between company logos.

| Connection | Meaning | Required evidence or qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Dated corporate or commercial relationship | An acquisition, agreement, deployment or integration is reported for a specific date and scope. | Named primary source; preserve historical scope and review current status before reliance. |
| Vendor compatibility claim | A supplier states that products can interoperate. | Exact products, versions and test conditions; not a COSMOSYNTH validation. |
| Analytical overlap | Organizations address comparable functions or competing alternatives. | Explain which function overlaps and where scope differs. |
| Hypothetical complementary interface | Capabilities might fit into an architecture. | An interface definition and test remain necessary; no existing collaboration is implied. |

## What a relationship record must preserve

Record both entity IDs, the relationship type, claim class, source date, source URL, product/version scope, uncertainty, last check and next validation. A 2025 memorandum must not become a 2026 exhibition agreement through an undated graph edge. A shared protocol family must not become a claim of interoperability without matching the actual profile and configuration.

**COSMOSYNTH relationships are a different record class.** Inclusion in this Atlas establishes neither a meeting nor a contract with us. Private introductions, individual contacts and conversations are not published. [System configurations](../SYSTEM_OPTIONS/README.md) show how this distinction informs engineering options.
