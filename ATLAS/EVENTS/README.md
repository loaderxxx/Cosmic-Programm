# Events are sources, not the architecture

[Atlas](../README.md) · [English](https://github.com/loaderxxx/Cosmic-Programm/blob/main/ATLAS/EVENTS/README.md) · [Русский](https://github.com/loaderxxx/Cosmic-Programm/blob/lang/ru/ATLAS/EVENTS/README.md) · [العربية](https://github.com/loaderxxx/Cosmic-Programm/blob/lang/ar/ATLAS/EVENTS/README.md)

The initial industry working set grew from CABSAT / SATExpo Dubai 2026 research. Its source catalogue preserves 51 SATExpo-linked or mentioned records and four CABSAT crossover records. It is a partial research sample, not a complete official exhibitor census or a log of meetings with COSMOSYNTH.

An event can help discover a company. It does not establish legal identity, current product capability, that a specific product was displayed, or a relationship with us. These dimensions retain separate evidence.
