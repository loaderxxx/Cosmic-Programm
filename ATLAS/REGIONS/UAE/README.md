## Why we study this ecosystem

We examine how institutions, missions, universities, companies, infrastructure and downstream services contribute to a sustained space sector. Our aim is to identify a narrow, credible research contribution grounded in sources and a measurable next step—not to present an unsolicited whole-programme funding request.

## Keep institutional roles separate

The existing [UAE actor register](../../../EN/RESEARCH/EXTERNAL_PROGRAMS/UAE_SPACE_PROGRAM/ACTOR_REGISTER_v0.1.md) treats UAE Space Agency, Mohammed bin Rashid Space Centre (MBRSC), universities and commercial organizations as distinct actors. A policy or sector-entry discussion is different from mission engineering, scientific review or procurement. A contact in one institution is not assumed to have authority for another.

| Reader | What to inspect | Possible question for a first discussion |
|---|---|---|
| UAE Space Agency or sector-development team | [Ecosystem study](../../../EN/RESEARCH/EXTERNAL_PROGRAMS/UAE_SPACE_PROGRAM/README.md), [company/capability Atlas](../../README.md), [research status](../../../RESEARCH_STATUS.md) | What contribution and participation route would be appropriate for an independent research initiative? |
| MBRSC or another mission-engineering team | [System options](../../SYSTEM_OPTIONS/README.md), [proposed pilot](../../../PILOTS/README.md), original mission requirements | Which non-confidential scenario and evidence would justify a technical review? |
| A university or laboratory | [Source records](../../DATA/README.md) and a bounded test concept | Can a reproducible question, baseline and reviewer be agreed? |
| A company or service provider | [Organization dossiers](../../ORGANIZATIONS/README.md) and [interface analysis](../../RELATIONSHIPS/README.md) | Which descriptions need correction, and what interface information can be discussed publicly? |

## IAC 2026 — new source-linked study

[UAE Space Pavilion evidence map](IAC_2026_PAVILION/README.md) covers 13 actors named by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs for IAC 2026, their publicly described capabilities, contract/project/institutional statements and four testable hypotheses. The [English evidence dataset](IAC_2026_PAVILION/ECOSYSTEM_DATA.json) keeps sources and relationship boundaries traceable. This is a separate sample, not an expansion of the CABSAT 55-record count or a claim of agency affiliation.

## What we can show now

An inspectable research library, an evidence-graded working industry catalogue, comparative capability analysis, eight conceptual system configurations and Mission A/B/C documents. These are evidence of research work, not evidence of flight heritage, customer validation or agency affiliation.

## A realistic first step

Select one problem, confirm who owns it, define the permitted inputs and agree what a useful result would look like. Programme eligibility, legal classification, access to facilities, data rights, procurement and funding remain matters for the relevant counterpart—not assumptions in this repository.

[Read the collaboration brief](../../../COLLABORATE.md). Individual contacts and meeting histories are private and are not published here. The catalogue's exhibition-derived records are a separate source set, not a complete inventory of the UAE space sector.
