# Cosmic Programm — Roadmap v0.1

## Phase 0 — Ground truth — 2026

- build evidence base
- map current space architectures
- map technology maturity
- identify hard physical limits
- establish legal/security boundaries
- build quantitative model infrastructure

## Phase 1 — Capability prototypes — 2026–2028

- AI research and engineering tooling
- autonomous robotics simulations
- ISRU process models
- power-system trade studies
- orbital logistics models
- mission architecture simulator
- economic model
- digital-twin concepts
- Earth-based prototypes

## Phase 2 — Lunar capability chain — planning horizon

- cargo/logistics
- power
- communications
- robotic excavation
- construction
- resource extraction
- mobility
- habitat systems
- industrial demonstrations

NASA currently treats lunar infrastructure as a proving ground for later Mars capabilities.

## Phase 3 — Sustained lunar economy

- repeatable logistics
- local resources
- power expansion
- maintenance
- manufacturing
- science
- commercial utilization

## Phase 4 — Mars preparation

- long-duration life support
- deep-space propulsion
- autonomous construction
- Mars cargo architecture
- surface power
- resource utilization
- return architecture
- human factors

NASA selected nuclear fission as the primary surface power generation technology for crewed Mars missions in its 2024 architecture decision.

## Phase 5 — Solar-system infrastructure

- asteroid scouting
- resource missions
- distributed communications
- deep-space power
- advanced propulsion
- autonomous industrial systems

## Phase 6 — Civilization-scale expansion

- multi-location industrial network
- large-scale orbital manufacturing
- permanent settlements
- interplanetary science network
- resilient knowledge/culture systems
- long-duration human/AI cooperation
