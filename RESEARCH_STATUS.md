## How the snapshot was used

The old catalogue PR is not silently merged with its historical README changes. [Data and provenance](ATLAS/DATA/README.md).

## Reading a claim

FACT describes supported evidence within a stated scope; SOURCE CLAIM attributes what a source says; ESTIMATE is a reasoned approximation; INTERPRETATION is analysis; HYPOTHESIS requires a test; UNKNOWN is unresolved. USER CLAIM is tracked separately when relevant and is not converted into a public affiliation claim.

A source-linked product is not independently qualified. A dated partnership announcement is not a current blanket authorization. A language edition does not turn an incomplete source into a fact.
