## Scope and quality state

The recovered package contains 35 programme documents, 31 technical-data documents, nine core SpaceX chapters and one cooperation study per language. These are **working research documents**, not flight qualification, supplier certification or a funded mission. This release preserves complete section-level translations where available and records outstanding checks rather than claiming that the whole historical repository is finished.

**Evidence date is not release date.** Historical prices, specifications and schedules retain their original context and require source revalidation before procurement or an external claim. Numerical screening, matching tables and working links do not establish scientific correctness or independent translation review. The detailed status is in the [release manifest](QUALITY/CS-TRI-20261010/manifest.json).

## How to interpret a relationship

The graph separates source-reported connections from hypotheses. An announcement, MoU, shipment, launch and accepted operational performance are different states. For each proposed combination ask: what capability is missing; which party could provide it; what interface, licence and acceptance evidence are required; what is the cheapest non-flight test? No named organization is represented as a COSMOSYNTH partner.

The original 8 October cooperation study described the CABSAT/SATExpo catalogue as an unmerged draft. The English 55-company catalogue is now published on `main`; its detailed RU/AR dossiers remain outside this completion claim.
