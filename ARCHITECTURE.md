# Cosmic Programm — Systems Architecture v0.1

## North Star

Enable progressively greater independent human and robotic activity beyond Earth while preserving safety, scientific value, economic viability, legal compliance and long-term resilience.

## Layer A — Earth foundation

Launch systems, manufacturing, energy, computation, communications, finance, workforce, research institutions and regulatory interfaces.

## Layer B — Access to space

Reusable launch, high cadence, payload standardization, rendezvous, docking, orbital servicing, propellant transfer and depots.

ESA's 2026 transportation programs explicitly study reusable launch systems, orbital logistics, refilling, maintenance, assembly and space tugs.

## Layer C — Orbital infrastructure

Communications, navigation, power, robotic servicing, manufacturing, laboratories, depots and commercial destinations.

## Layer D — Lunar infrastructure

Landing/cargo, power, communications, navigation, autonomous excavation, construction, water/oxygen extraction, mobility, habitats and logistics.

NASA identifies power, water, ISRU, construction and autonomous robotics as foundational lunar technologies.

## Layer E — Deep-space transport

High-efficiency propulsion, radiation protection, life support, logistics, navigation, communications and autonomous operations.

## Layer F — Mars infrastructure

Power, landing/cargo, autonomous construction, resource extraction, food/life support, habitation, mobility, manufacturing and return logistics.

## Layer G — Solar-system expansion

Asteroid reconnaissance, resource utilization, outer-system robotics, advanced propulsion, distributed infrastructure and scientific observatories.

## Layer H — Civilization-scale systems

Resilient governance, interoperable standards, distributed manufacturing, knowledge preservation, multi-generation planning, planetary stewardship and human/AI cooperation.

## Cross-cutting systems

POWER: solar, storage, fission and future energy systems.
AUTONOMY: supervised autonomy progressing toward higher independence.
RESOURCES: progressively replace Earth-supplied mass where technically and economically justified.
MANUFACTURING: local production where launch mass and resilience justify it.
AI: planning, perception, fault diagnosis, scheduling, science and human-machine coordination with verification.

## Architecture invariant

A new capability should increase future option space rather than lock the program into one narrow path.

## Compounding principle

POWER + ROBOTICS + LOGISTICS + RESOURCES + HABITATION should be reusable across Moon, Mars and later destinations.

## Decision record

Every major architecture decision records:
DECISION → ALTERNATIVES → ASSUMPTIONS → EVIDENCE → TRADE-OFFS → FAILURE MODES → REVERSIBILITY → REVIEW DATE.
