# COSMOSYNTH — Independent Space Research & Evidence Mapping
**Audience:** UAE Space Agency (potential introductory discussion)  
**Language:** English | **Format:** seven-slide / approximately five-minute content skeleton  
**Status:** PUBLIC DRAFT — NOT A COMPLETED OR DELIVERED DECK  
**Date:** 2026-10-08

## Slide 1 — COSMOSYNTH: independent research, traceable outputs
**Core statement:** We are developing an independent space research programme focused on turning complex public information into reliable research briefs, capability maps and testable opportunities.

- Research-first, exploratory / systems R&D stage.
- Independent; no implied endorsement by the UAE Space Agency, MBRSC or another space organisation.
- Demonstrable output today: research documents and structured evidence registers, not flight hardware or deployed mission systems.

**Speaker note:** Describe the team and legal entity only when independently confirmed. Do not claim an organization size, accredited institute status, contract portfolio or proven technology readiness.

## Slide 2 — The information problem we help solve
Space-sector decisions span institutions, missions, suppliers, regulations, datasets, technical interfaces and changing programme timelines.

**Proposed value:**
- Convert dispersed public sources into an auditable research inventory.
- Map **who does what**, which capabilities are documented, and where interactions may be relevant.
- Separate documented facts, source claims, interpretation, hypotheses and unknowns.
- Surface duplication, dependency, evidence gaps and candidate areas for further validation.

**Deliverables:** linked source register, organisational/mission map, capability matrix, updated analytical brief.

## Slide 3 — Our working method
**QUESTION → COLLECT → ATTRIBUTE → VERIFY → NORMALISE → CROSS-ANALYSE → MAP → SYNTHESISE → REVIEW**

- Each important statement links to its original source, date and maturity/status.
- Conflicts and gaps stay visible rather than being smoothed over.
- Automation and AI support speed of collection and synthesis; independent checking and human review protect quality.
- We can produce bilingual research outputs in English and Arabic, with substantive terminology review before external use.

**Do not claim:** any guaranteed turnaround time, exhaustive coverage, automated access to nonpublic databases or a certified accuracy metric.

## Slide 5 — Relevance to the UAE space ecosystem
**SOURCE CLAIM:** The UAE Cabinet approved National Space Strategy 2031 on 29 March 2026, identifying investment attractiveness, international partnerships/market access, and competitive infrastructure among its objectives [S02]. The Agency publicly identifies sector development, research, partnerships, applications and an enabling legal framework as part of its mandate [S01].

**Interpretation:** an independent, source-traceable research layer *might* help scope opportunities and expose technical/market interfaces for review. It would complement, not replace or duplicate, existing Agency systems.

**Question:** Which clearly bounded evidence-mapping task would actually be useful to a relevant Agency team or its ecosystem partners?

## Slide 6 — Proposal: one bounded public-data research demonstration
**HYPOTHESIS, not an agreed pilot:** Select a single, nonconfidential topic, for example the UAE Earth-observation downstream ecosystem or an openly documented mission-supplier landscape.

**Proposed output:**
1. Agreed research question and scope.
2. Public, licit sources with attribution and update dates.
3. Evidence-graded actor/capability/relationship matrix.
4. Contradictions, blind spots and actionable follow-up questions.
5. Short bilingual results brief and a reproducibility sample.

**Suggested acceptance criteria for discussion:** source coverage within the agreed scope, traceability of material claims, error rate on a jointly reviewed sample, update effort and usefulness to the reviewing stakeholder. No targets or delivery dates are claimed yet.

## Slide 7 — Specific request and next step
We would welcome a brief scoping discussion to identify:
- a nonconfidential research question that matters to the Agency or a suitable ecosystem programme;
- the correct technical or innovation point of contact;
- a useful output format and a simple review criterion.
