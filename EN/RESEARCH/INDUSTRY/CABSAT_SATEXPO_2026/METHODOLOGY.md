# Research methods and safeguards

**Reviewed 8 October 2026.** Publicly visible working directory on a research branch, not an official exhibitor census or supplier certification.

## Data provenance

- 51 initial SATExpo-linked/mentioned entities plus 4 separate CABSAT crossover leads. Original identity is preserved through stable P/C IDs.
- Exhibitor status is separated from current product-line claims. A 2026 official logo is not proof that a particular listed product was on the stand.
- 40 company entries are linked to primary manufacturer/organization product references; others have preliminary, event-derived or unresolved identification only.
- Every public record has at least a source of its original event/index inclusion (or a documented ambiguity).

## Relationship epistemology

- **FACT or dated source report:** corporate acquisition, signed MoU, product integration or published service; scope/date must stay attached.
- **SOURCE CLAIM:** vendor asserts interoperability or supplier-client relation; do not imply independent confirmation.
- **INTERPRETATION:** comparable capability classes; no contract or technical compatibility asserted.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a possible subsystem boundary or future study; not a commitment.
- **UNKNOWN:** unverified identity/attendance, technical specs, product/version availability, regulatory status and all COSMOSYNTH relationships.

## Safe disclosures

No private contacts, internal opportunity rankings, negotiations, proprietary mission designs, privileged technical data, pricing hypotheses, letters or secrets. No photos or official brand logos copied.

## Corrections held in the record

- Es'hailSat confirmed CABSAT 2026 attendance; announced booth/hall requires reconciliation between company and organiser layouts.
- Neo Space Group, Türksat, Azercosmos, Nilesat and Kratos have relevant independently described technologies but their **2026 exhibitor presence remains unconfirmed** in this source set.
- Geoscan is a manufacturer of CubeSat platforms, not merely an aerial mapping provider; Coxsat and Mikwave list phased-array satellite technologies.

## Publication and governance

This branch is publicly accessible by design. Merge into main, public promotion, sending profile links to counterparties and requests for supplier cooperation require a separate project-owner decision.

All named organizations, logos, trademarks and business claims belong to their owners; company claims are cited, not independently endorsed.
