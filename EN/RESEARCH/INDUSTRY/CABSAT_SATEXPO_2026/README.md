# COSMOSYNTH | Satellite Industry Capability Atlas

### CABSAT / SATExpo Dubai · 5–7 October 2026

An independent, evidence-graded directory of **55 publicly identified organizations or brand labels** relevant to satellite communications, space systems, ground infrastructure and adjacent technical services. This is a **partial research sample**, not the official or complete exhibitor list.

Each entity has its own folder with a public profile, capability decomposition, industry connections, primary-source register and a machine-readable record. Some entries are screening-only and are explicitly flagged until their identities or event role are confirmed.

## What this atlas demonstrates

- Capability mapping rather than a logo collection: mission software, orbital capacity, ground stations, terminals, RF subsystems, Digital IF, satellite data and industry organisations.
- Published company-to-company relations are separated from our own technical hypotheses.
- Potential multi-company architectures are examples for discussion, **not existing joint products or vendor endorsement**.

### How to read

Start with an entity profile, review its source links, examine confirmed industry ties and compare its system layer with adjacent companies. The technical fit of any proposed combination requires separate vendor/API/spectrum evaluation.
