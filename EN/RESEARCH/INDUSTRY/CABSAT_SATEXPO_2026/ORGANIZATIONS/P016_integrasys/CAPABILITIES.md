## System boundaries

- **RF and/or optical link engineering**: Geometry, radio budgets, coverage and link conditions. Before integration, obtain model/version, interface/control documentation, supported operating environment and independent tests.
- **Multi-orbit and network resource orchestration**: Resource allocation, carrier/routing decisions, ground contacts. Before integration, obtain model/version, interface/control documentation, supported operating environment and independent tests.
- **Digital intermediate frequency**: RF-over-IP IQ streams, packet timing, contextual metadata and interoperability. Before integration, obtain model/version, interface/control documentation, supported operating environment and independent tests.
- **Spectrum and network assurance**: Signal monitoring, faults and operational observability. Before integration, obtain model/version, interface/control documentation, supported operating environment and independent tests.

## Interoperability cannot be inferred from a sector label

- A satellite operator's orbit/capacity service is distinct from satellite modem hardware and mission flight software.
- Products with common labels (VSAT, phased array, Digital IF, GEO/LEO) may have different frequencies, protocols, licences, environments and API profiles.
- No equipment, firmware, API, SLA, orbit availability, funding or flight-qualification claim has been independently tested or procured by COSMOSYNTH.
