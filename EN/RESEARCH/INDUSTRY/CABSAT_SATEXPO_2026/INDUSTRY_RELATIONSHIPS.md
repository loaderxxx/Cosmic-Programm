## Illustrative interfaces to research (NOT established joint products)

- **Mission software ↔ ground scheduling:** Antaris (mission virtualisation) + Calian (resource orchestration) + AWS Ground Station (managed station scheduling). The companies are not claimed to operate a common integration.
- **Digital ground signal chain:** ETL Systems (RF/Digital IF) + INTEGRASYS (spectrum signal analysis) + an appropriate demodulation stack (Amphinicy Blink as ETL group company). DIFI source claims need packet-level verification.
- **VSAT connectivity:** satellite operator/service footprint (SES, Eutelsat, AsiaSat, etc.) + baseband/VSAT hub (iDirect, Comtech, Hughes, ND SatCom, SpaceBridge) + licensed antenna terminal (AvL/SATEC/StarWin etc.). This is a generic stack, not a turnkey proposal.
- **Small satellite + EO pipeline:** Geoscan 16U spacecraft/imagery capability + independently evaluated mission/ground operations and data ingestion. No vendor partnership inferred.

Documented relationship, vendor statement, technical complement and competitive overlap must not be collapsed into one label. See each organisation's RELATIONSHIPS.md for the precise source and scope.
