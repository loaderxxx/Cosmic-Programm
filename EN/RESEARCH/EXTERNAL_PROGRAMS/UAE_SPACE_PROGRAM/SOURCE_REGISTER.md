## Verification rules
FACT = directly supported by official documentation; SOURCE CLAIM = source's own forward-looking or promotional assertion; INTERPRETATION = independent analysis; HYPOTHESIS = proposition to test; UNKNOWN = unresolved. Historical source dates must not be mistaken for present-day status. Record source URL, publication date, access date, revision, and conflicting evidence for each numerical or status claim.
