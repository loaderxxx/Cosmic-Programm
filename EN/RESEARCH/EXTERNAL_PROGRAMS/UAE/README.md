# United Arab Emirates — National Space Programme | Systems Research

**Date:** 2026-10-08 | **Version:** 0.1 | **Status:** public, preliminary, evidence-tracked  
**Languages:** [English](README.md) · [Русский](../../../../RU/RESEARCH/EXTERNAL_PROGRAMS/UAE/README.md) · [العربية](../../../../AR/RESEARCH/EXTERNAL_PROGRAMS/UAE/README.md)

**Scope:** The UAE national space programme, commercial and university sectors, international interfaces and enabling regulation; not merely one government agency. COSMOSYNTH is an independent initiative and is **not affiliated** with the UAE Space Agency, MBRSC, NASA, or the other organisations studied.

## The research question

What institutional, technical, commercial and learning mechanisms allow the UAE space ecosystem to acquire, integrate, operate and expand real capabilities — and which of those mechanisms, if evidenced, can inform an independently validated COSMOSYNTH concept?

Our starting hypothesis is **mission programmes → engineering talent and integration → operational data/services → domestic industry and new missions**. This is **INTERPRETATION**, not an official UAE architecture or a proven economic causal model.

## How this reproduces the SpaceX research method

The inherited process is **COLLECT → REGISTER → EXTRACT → NORMALIZE → VERIFY → COMPARE → SYNTHESIZE → REQUIREMENTS → TASKS → UPDATE**, from [COSMOSYNTH's existing research pipeline](../../../../RESEARCH/ANALYSIS_PIPELINE_v1.0.md). The SpaceX study decomposes a commercial vertically integrated system; the UAE study applies the same capability/architecture/interface/economics/evidence lens to a **multi-actor national system**.

Each claim must state its evidence category: **FACT** (verifiable institutional event/document), **SOURCE CLAIM** (institutional self-report), **INTERPRETATION**, **ESTIMATE**, **HYPOTHESIS**, or **UNKNOWN**. Every mission additionally gets a status: operating, completed, developing, proposed, replanned or unverified.

## Timeline: accomplished versus targeted

- **2009 / 2013:** DubaiSat-1 / DubaiSat-2 launched; institutional history describes South Korean cooperation and transition of engineering capabilities [S35].
- **2014:** UAE Space Agency established [S01].
- **2018:** KhalifaSat launched; MBRSC describes it as designed and manufactured domestically [S35].
- **2020:** Emirates Mars Mission / Hope launched; UAE among the eight original Artemis Accords signatories [S18, S39].
- **February 2021:** Hope entered Mars orbit [S18].
- **March–September 2023:** astronaut Sultan AlNeyadi flew a six-month ISS mission; the earlier Rashid-1 lunar lander attempt did not achieve a soft landing [S36, S37].
- **2024:** NASA and the UAE announced a future Gateway crew and science airlock contribution [S23]; **the future programme changed**.
- **January / March 2025:** MBZ-SAT and Etihad-SAT launched [S19, S20]. The EMA team also announced completion of its spacecraft CDR [S15].
- **March 2026:** NASA stated it would **pause Gateway in its then-current form**, focusing instead on a Moon Base programme [S24]. The Emirati airlock's final disposition is **UNKNOWN**, not categorically cancelled.
- **2026:** Official communications invoke **National Space Strategy 2031** rather than only the earlier Strategy 2030; UAE Space Agency states Hope will operate through 2028 [S03, S18].
- **March 2028 target:** UAE Space Agency's prospective launch window for the MBR Explorer asteroid mission — **NOT a completed launch** [S14].
- **2117 vision:** the previously stated Mars human-settlement vision is **long-term aspiration**, not a funded/validated construction schedule [S38].

## Technical capability map

1. **Earth observation:** optical and radar payloads, AOCS, on-board data handling, RF downlink, ground processing and distribution. Compare latency, calibrated geolocation, coverage, revisit, price and verified detection accuracy — not press-release resolution figures alone.
2. **Space telecommunications:** GEO/mobile services and growing terrestrial/NTN integration. Trace payload frequency bands, ITU/orbital filings, spectrum permissions, ground hubs, terminal capabilities and measured service uptime.
3. **Deep-space systems:** Mars science operations, asteroid mission navigation, power, radiation/thermal design, propulsion, mission operations and fault-management. Maintain distinct TRL and verification states per subsystem.
4. **Lunar and human spaceflight:** rover/lander and human-rated interfaces, environmental testing and operations. Gateway's 2026 redesign is an explicit programme dependency, not a cosmetic schedule change.
5. **Manufacturing / AIT:** study facility instrumentation, traceable cleanroom qualification, EMC, TVAC, vibration, integration, supplier interfaces and export controls. A national workshare *target* is not proof of local IP ownership.
6. **AI/data systems:** scientific/EO datasets and analytics are potential publicly reproducible benchmarks; AI-generated products are not evidence of in-flight spacecraft autonomy.
7. **Launch services / ground segment:** the studied satellite launches used international launch services. National indigenous orbital-launch maturity has **NOT BEEN EXHAUSTIVELY VERIFIED**. Distinguish ground station, launch range and launch vehicle.

## Funding, economy and regulatory boundary

- **SOURCE CLAIM**: the UAE National Space Fund is **AED 3 billion** [S05]. Neither disbursement history nor availability to a specific applicant is established.
- **SOURCE CLAIM**: the EMA agency page states **50% of work** is allocated to UAE private-sector companies [S14]. We have not audited executed procurement, paid invoices, or value added.
- Space Economic Zones and a 2025 Space Economic Survey provide ecosystem and measurement frameworks [S06, S41]. Do not make up sector GDP, investment or employment totals.
- Federal Decree-Law **46 of 2023** and related 2025 regulations are the legal baseline [S08,S09]; a **90-day regularisation notice issued 27 July 2026** is material for entities operating or intending space activities [S10]. Applicability for a particular COSMOSYNTH activity needs regulatory/legal determination.
- The UAE is a founding signatory of the **Artemis Accords** [S39]. These are civil cooperation principles and not a partnership or contract with COSMOSYNTH.

## What may transfer to COSMOSYNTH

**HYPOTHESES FOR TESTING, NOT DESIGN APPROVALS:**
- A **mission-led industrial learning loop** may accelerate local subsystem design and system-integration capability. Verify with actual before/after supplier responsibility, test acceptance and engineer retention records.
- **Data-product first pilots** may reduce entry costs relative to flight hardware. Verify public licensing, datasets, independent ground truth and repeatability.
- **Interface ownership through partnerships** may be more useful at small scale than duplicating every subsystem. Test through a bounded Mission A autonomy/FDIR simulator or EO-data benchmark.

**Three independently evaluable work packages:**
A. Validate a public optical/SAR observation pipeline against held-out ground truth.  
B. Simulate spacecraft autonomous recovery under limited power and delayed communications.  
C. Produce an end-to-end requirements / source-evidence / schedule traceability matrix for EMA.
