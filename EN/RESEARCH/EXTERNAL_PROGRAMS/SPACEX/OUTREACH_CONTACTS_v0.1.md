# SpaceX / SpaceXAI — Public Contact and Outreach Routes

Date: 2026-09-22  
Version: v0.1  
Status: PUBLIC RESEARCH / CONTACT MAP

## Purpose

This file records public contact routes that may be relevant for a future introduction to the SpaceX / SpaceXAI ecosystem.

Only publicly published organizational addresses are listed here.

No private or inferred email addresses are included.

## 1. Primary SpaceX route — Media

media@spacex.com

Use case:
- a concise public-research introduction;
- request that the material be routed internally to an appropriate AI/engineering contact;
- public research / press context.

Source: SpaceX investor-relations releases list Media as media@spacex.com.

## 2. SpaceX commercial / launch sales

sales@spacex.com

Use case:
- commercial/business inquiries related to SpaceX launch services.

This is not an AI research mailbox, so it should be treated as a secondary routing channel.

## 3. SpaceX Rideshare

rideshare@spacex.com

Use case:
- payload / rideshare / launch-related inquiries.

This becomes relevant only if the research eventually includes an actual payload or orbital experiment.

## 4. SpaceX Investor Relations

investors@spacex.com

Use case:
- investor / corporate-information route.

Not a technical-collaboration mailbox. Useful only as a corporate routing channel when the topic overlaps with publicly disclosed AI strategy.

## 5. SpaceX privacy/legal

privacy@spacex.com

This address is publicly published for privacy/legal requests.

It is not recommended for collaboration outreach.

## 6. SpaceXAI / xAI product support

support@x.ai

The public xAI contact page states that its contact routes cover questions about Grok, enterprise support and partnership exploration.

Use case:
- AI/product context;
- asking to route a research proposal to an appropriate SpaceXAI/xAI team.

## 7. SpaceXAI / xAI media

media@x.ai

Use case:
- media/publication context.

## 8. xAI government route

global-government@x.ai

This is specifically published for government transformative-project inquiries.

Not appropriate for ordinary independent-research outreach.

## 9. xAI safety

safety@x.ai

Not appropriate for collaboration outreach.

Recorded only as an official public route.

## 10. What was not found

I did not find a reliable publicly published general-purpose email specifically labeled:
- SpaceX AI research partnerships;
- SpaceX engineering research collaboration;
- SpaceXAI ↔ independent researchers.

No address of that type is invented here.

Practical routing:

public research link → official public contact route → request internal forwarding to the relevant AI / engineering / research team.

## 11. Recommended first-contact set

For an initial outreach attempt, the most defensible public routes are:

media@spacex.com

and, for the AI side:

support@x.ai

A route such as sales@spacex.com should be used only when the proposal has a commercial launch or product dimension.
