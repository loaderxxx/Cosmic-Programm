# SpaceX — Vertical Integration

Date: **2026-09-22**  
Version: **v0.1**

## What is documented

SpaceX publicly describes a very high degree of vertical integration and associates it with speed and cost control. In 2026 corporate materials, SpaceX described approximately 80% of Starship as being produced in-house. This is a **company statement**, not an independent Cosmic Programm audit.

## What vertical integration can change

Potential benefits:
- supplier latency;
- design handoff latency;
- interface count;
- procurement dependencies;
- modification lead time.

Potential costs:
- fixed costs;
- capital intensity;
- internal coordination;
- concentration risk;
- organizational coupling.

## The useful question

Not:

**“Should we build everything ourselves?”**

But:

**“Which interfaces are so critical to speed, safety, IP or reliability that internal control creates system value?”**

## Cosmic Programm framework

For each critical interface:

**criticality → change frequency → supplier latency → switching cost → IP sensitivity → make/buy/partner decision**

This turns Build/Buy into an engineering decision rather than a doctrine.

## Deeper insight

Vertical integration is fundamentally a way of managing **time, interfaces and change latency**, not simply a way of increasing the percentage of parts made in-house.

## Open questions

- Full internal cost of ownership;
- true supplier dependencies;
- make/buy boundary by subsystem;
- resilience benefits;
- minimum vertical integration required by a smaller program.
