## Purpose

This section contains the English-language public research track of Cosmic Programm focused on SpaceX.

SpaceX is treated as an external benchmark and source of evidence, not as the canonical architecture of Cosmic Programm.

## Research question

**Which system-level mechanisms allow SpaceX to design, build, test, launch, operate, and scale space capabilities — and which of those mechanisms can be transferred, with evidence, into Cosmic Programm?**

## Publication boundary

This public track contains publishable research, sources, analysis, conclusions and open questions.

It does not publish:
- private prompts;
- private algorithms;
- Private Core contents;
- credentials or secrets;
- private contacts;
- protected infrastructure details;
- unpublished sensitive IP details that have not passed publication review.

## Important provenance rule

Company claims, job postings, investor disclosures and independent oversight are not equivalent evidence.

In particular:
- a **job posting** demonstrates a hiring/building intent or an existing team requirement, but does not by itself prove broad production deployment;
- a **company disclosure** documents what SpaceX says about its system;
- an **independent oversight source** provides external validation or challenge;
- a **research interpretation** remains a hypothesis until corroborated.
