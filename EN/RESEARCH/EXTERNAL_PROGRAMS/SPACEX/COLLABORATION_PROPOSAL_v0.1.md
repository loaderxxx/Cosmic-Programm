# SpaceX ↔ Independent Researcher Collaboration Proposal

Date: 2026-09-22  
Version: v0.1  
Status: PUBLIC / WORKING PROPOSAL

## Purpose

This document is a starting point for a possible future conversation between SpaceX / SpaceXAI and an independent systems researcher building a long-term cognitive and engineering research system.

It is intentionally exploratory.

No claim is made of an existing relationship, endorsement, partnership or representation of SpaceX.

## 1. Who I am

I am an independent software architect, researcher and systems builder.

I am developing a personal cognitive layer for my own mind: a structured, provider-independent system that combines memory, research, reasoning, agents, tools, provenance, experiments and human control.

The objective is not simply to build an assistant.

The objective is to build a system that can continuously:

observe → learn → reason → test → remember → act → evaluate → improve

I am applying the same systems thinking to a long-term public research program focused on human and robotic expansion beyond Earth.

## 2. Why SpaceX

While studying SpaceX, I found a striking convergence between several things I am independently trying to build:

short engineering feedback loops

capability-centric architecture

control of critical interfaces

high-throughput experimentation

physical + digital integration

large-scale data loops

AI agents connected to real tools

infrastructure that compounds with use

This does not mean my system is equivalent to SpaceX.

It means SpaceX is a highly relevant external benchmark for questions I care deeply about.

DOCUMENTED FACT

## 4. Why a conversation could be useful

I am interested in exchanging questions and evidence, not asking for confidential information.

A possible collaboration could explore:

### AI for engineering systems

How can AI agents be connected safely to:
- engineering knowledge;
- simulations;
- test systems;
- software repositories;
- telemetry;
- manufacturing data?

### Cognitive infrastructure

How should a long-lived engineering organization build a durable cognitive layer that survives individual models and vendors?

### Human + AI systems

How should autonomy, permissions, provenance, verification and human approval be combined?

### Space + AI

How does large-scale physical infrastructure change the architecture of intelligent systems?

### Public research

Some questions can be explored openly through public evidence without touching proprietary SpaceX information.

## 5. What I can contribute

I can contribute:
- systems architecture thinking;
- AI-agent architecture;
- provider-independent cognitive systems;
- experiment design;
- public technical synthesis;
- software architecture;
- structured provenance and knowledge systems;
- long-term research methodology.

I am comfortable starting from a very small, well-defined technical question and expanding only when value is demonstrated.

## 6. What I am not asking for

I am not asking SpaceX to disclose:
- proprietary algorithms;
- confidential engineering data;
- internal model weights;
- trade secrets;
- private roadmaps;
- private customer information;
- security-sensitive details.

The intent is to find a legitimate boundary where independent research can be useful without creating disclosure risk.

## 7. Possible starting point

A simple first conversation could be about one narrow question:

How should a high-throughput engineering organization build the cognitive layer that connects knowledge, models, agents, tools, tests and operational data?

I have started building such a layer for my own cognitive system and am studying SpaceX because your public work appears to address adjacent questions at a very large physical scale.

## 8. Long-term possibility

If there is technical alignment, collaboration could eventually take forms such as:
- research exchange;
- technical discussion;
- public non-confidential research;
- software/agent experiments;
- engineering knowledge-system experiments;
- joint exploration of human/AI engineering workflows.

The scope should be defined by evidence and mutual interest.

## 9. Working principle

No hype. No assumption of access. No request for secrets.

Start with:

one question → one experiment → one measurable result → next question.

## 10. Closing

I am building a cognitive layer for my own mind and a long-term public research program around space systems.

I see SpaceX as one of the most interesting organizations studying many adjacent problems at extreme scale.

I would be interested in a technical conversation if someone on the relevant SpaceX / SpaceXAI side finds the research useful.
