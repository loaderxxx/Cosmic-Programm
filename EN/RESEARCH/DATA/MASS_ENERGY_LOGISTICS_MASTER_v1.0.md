# Mass, Energy and Logistics Master Dataset v1.0

## Mass budget

Every mission must account for:
- dry mass;
- propellant;
- payload;
- structural margin;
- cables/piping;
- thermal insulation;
- adapters;
- mass uncertainty.

The budget must include a reserve, rather than closing exactly at zero margin.

## Power and energy budget

Distinguish:
- average power;
- peak power;
- inrush currents;
- battery charge;
- eclipse mode;
- safe mode;
- degradation of the power source.

## Logistics budget

For each mission, calculate:
- mass delivered from Earth;
- volume;
- number of launches;
- delivery time;
- ground operations;
- storage;
- spare elements;
- communications;
- operator time;
- dependence on external suppliers.

## Programme-level rule

Optimize not the individual spacecraft, but the cost and complexity of the entire chain:

manufacturing → integration → launch → transportation → operations → data → servicing → next mission.

## Cosmic

Every mission must have three budgets:
**mass + energy + data**, connected to the schedule and logistics.
