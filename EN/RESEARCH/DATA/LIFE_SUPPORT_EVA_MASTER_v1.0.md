# Life Support and EVA v1.0

## Life support

Track:
- O2 generation;
- CO2 removal;
- water recovery;
- humidity;
- trace contaminants;
- thermal rejection;
- food;
- waste;
- spares;
- failure tolerance.

## EVA

Track:
- pressure;
- mobility;
- thermal control;
- radiation;
- dust;
- consumables;
- communications;
- suit maintenance;
- emergency return.

## Principal metric

Not just nominal performance, but the probability of safely completing a long-duration operation under component degradation.

## Significance

Crewed Mars architecture requires a substantially higher level of evidence than robotic missions.
