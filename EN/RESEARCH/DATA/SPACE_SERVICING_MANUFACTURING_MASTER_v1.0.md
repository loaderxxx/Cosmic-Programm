# Orbital Servicing and Space Manufacturing v1.0

## Servicing

Functions:
- inspection;
- docking;
- refuelling;
- repair;
- replacement;
- debris avoidance;
- relocation;
- life extension.

## Manufacturing

Classes:
- additive manufacturing;
- fibre/composites;
- metals;
- glass;
- biological/chemical processes;
- semiconductor/science payloads.

## Programme principle

Manufacturing is worthwhile if it reduces launched mass, supply-chain dependence or maintenance burden.

## Metrics

- feedstock mass;
- power;
- throughput;
- product quality;
- waste;
- autonomous labour;
- maintenance;
- delivered value per kg launched.
