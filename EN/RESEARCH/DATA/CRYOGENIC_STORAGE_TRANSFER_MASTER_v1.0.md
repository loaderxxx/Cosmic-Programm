# Cryogenic Storage and Transfer v1.0

## Purpose

Study one of the central elements of lunar and Martian logistics: long-duration storage and transfer of cryogenic propellants.

## Chain

production → liquefaction → storage → boil-off control → measurement → transfer → refuelling → mass confirmation.

## Parameters

- propellant type;
- temperature;
- pressure;
- tank mass;
- insulation;
- heat leak;
- boil-off;
- venting;
- transfer rate;
- coupling;
- contamination;
- metering;
- leak detection.

## System significance

Cryogenic infrastructure links ISRU, transportation, orbital depots, lunar logistics and Mars architecture.

## Verification

Required:
- long-duration ground tests;
- thermal-vacuum tests;
- transfer tests;
- leak tests;
- sensor calibration;
- fault injection.
