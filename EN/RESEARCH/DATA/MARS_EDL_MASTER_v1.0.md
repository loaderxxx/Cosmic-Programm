# Mars Entry, Descent and Landing (EDL) v1.0

## Chain

entry → thermal protection → aerodynamic deceleration → guidance → terminal descent → landing → surface activation.

## Principal constraints

- atmosphere;
- mass;
- ballistic coefficient;
- thermal load;
- parachute applicability;
- propulsion;
- terrain hazards;
- landing dispersion;
- communications delay;
- autonomous decision-making.

## Verification

Combine:
- atmospheric simulation;
- trajectory Monte Carlo analysis;
- thermal analysis;
- hardware tests;
- drop/free-flight tests where applicable;
- integrated software/hardware simulation;
- flight data.

## Programme connection

Mars EDL must be a separate technology track long before the first large Mars mission.
