# Lunar Navigation and Relay v1.0

## Purpose

A positioning, navigation and timing (PNT) / relay system for autonomous spacecraft and future infrastructure.

## Components

- orbiting relay;
- surface nodes;
- direct-to-Earth links;
- optical/RF ranging;
- onboard navigation;
- terrain-relative navigation;
- time distribution.

## Metrics

- position error;
- velocity error;
- time error;
- coverage;
- latency;
- availability;
- integrity;
- continuity;
- terminal mass/power;
- redundancy.

## Engineering principle

Navigation should be treated as an infrastructure service, not merely as a sensor on one spacecraft.
