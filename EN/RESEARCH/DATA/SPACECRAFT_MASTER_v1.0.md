# Spacecraft Master Dataset v1.0

## Engineering schema

For every spacecraft, record:
- purpose;
- orbit/trajectory;
- dry and launch mass;
- dimensions;
- power system;
- batteries;
- propulsion;
- attitude-control system;
- navigation;
- communications;
- computing system;
- thermal management;
- radiation protection;
- payload;
- service life;
- fault-tolerant modes;
- ground segment.

## Orion

Purpose: crew transportation between Earth and deep-space trajectories, and return.

The architecture includes a crew module, a European Service Module and a launch abort system.

## Gateway

Purpose: modular infrastructure in cislunar space.

Functions include habitation, logistics, science, communications and support for lunar operations.

## Cosmic spacecraft

The first generation prioritizes gaining engineering experience on a small autonomous spacecraft rather than developing a crewed vehicle:
1. power;
2. computing;
3. communications;
4. attitude control;
5. autonomy;
6. payload;
7. telemetry;
8. fault tolerance.

This should establish a foundation for progressively more complex missions.
