# Lunar Power and Nuclear Sources v1.0

## Purpose

Systematize power as foundational infrastructure for long-duration lunar operations.

## Classes

- solar generation;
- batteries;
- DC distribution;
- local microgrids;
- radioisotope sources;
- surface nuclear power systems;
- hybrid architectures.

## Engineering parameters

For each option, record:
- continuous power;
- peak power;
- mass;
- area;
- efficiency;
- storage;
- service life;
- degradation;
- thermal rejection;
- deployment complexity;
- radiation requirements;
- redundancy;
- maintenance requirements.

## Lunar cases

Analyse separately:
1. illuminated sites;
2. shadowed sites;
3. long-duration eclipse;
4. polar operating conditions;
5. mobile consumers;
6. permanent infrastructure.

## Rule

The power-source choice must follow the mission power profile and environment, not a preference for a technology.
