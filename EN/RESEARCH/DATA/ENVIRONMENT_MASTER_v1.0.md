# Space Environments Master Dataset v1.0

## Purpose

A common physical-environment requirements layer for comparing orbital, lunar and deep-space missions.

## Environments

### Earth orbit
- vacuum;
- atomic oxygen at applicable altitudes;
- radiation;
- thermal cycles;
- micrometeoroids and orbital debris;
- dynamic loads.

### Moon
- vacuum;
- extreme temperature cycles;
- regolith;
- dust;
- radiation;
- micrometeoroids;
- low gravity;
- long periods of illumination/darkness.

### Deep space
- radiation environment;
- solar radiation;
- communications delay;
- limited power availability;
- long-duration autonomy;
- no rapid physical servicing capability.

## Parameters for each scenario

**environment → range → duration → source → uncertainty → subsystem impact → verification method.**

## Principle

The environment must enter requirements before component selection, rather than being used as a check after design.
