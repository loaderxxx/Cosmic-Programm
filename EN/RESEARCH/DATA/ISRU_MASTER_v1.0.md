# ISRU Master Dataset v1.0

ISRU means using local resources to reduce dependence on supplies from Earth.

## Candidate resource classes

- water/ice;
- oxygen from regolith;
- metals;
- silicate materials;
- regolith as a construction feedstock;
- volatiles.

## Process chain

prospecting → resource confirmation → extraction → raw-material transport → processing → purification → storage → use.

## Engineering rule

The presence of an assumed resource does not establish economic or technical suitability. Before selecting a technology, measure:
- concentration;
- spatial heterogeneity;
- burial depth;
- accessibility;
- extraction energy cost;
- equipment requirements;
- losses;
- product yield;
- reliability;
- degradation modes.

## Cosmic

ISRU is introduced after resource prospecting. The initial task is to obtain measurement data, not to select a specific extraction technology in advance.
