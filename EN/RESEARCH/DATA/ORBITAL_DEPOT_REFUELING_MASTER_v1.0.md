# Orbital Propellant Depots and Refuelling v1.0

## Architecture

launch → depot → storage → tanker → transfer → customer vehicle.

## Functions

- storage;
- propellant receipt;
- measurement;
- thermal control;
- transfer;
- docking;
- leak isolation;
- fault recovery;
- inventory management.

## Key unknowns

- infrastructure mass fraction;
- boil-off;
- operations frequency;
- docking reliability;
- contamination;
- connection standardization;
- operational turnaround;
- economics.

## Significance

If refuelling becomes repeatable, not only transportation but the architecture of the entire programme changes: the transportation vehicle can be separated from the energy/logistics reserve.
