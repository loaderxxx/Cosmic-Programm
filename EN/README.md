## Welcome
COSMOSYNTH / Cosmic Programm is an **independent exploratory space-systems research initiative**, not an operational launch provider or government space agency. We investigate how missions, infrastructure, science, industrial capabilities, regulatory systems, skills and commercial services work together.

Our goals are to **identify credible evidence, map relationships between capabilities, test engineering hypotheses and turn research into actionable, measurable experiments**.

## Analytical method
Collect → register → extract → normalize → verify → compare → synthesize → convert verified needs into requirements and tasks → test → update.

Classification matters: **FACT** (documented), **SOURCE CLAIM** (what an institution says), **ESTIMATE** (calculated), **HYPOTHESIS** (to test), **INTERPRETATION** (reasoned view), **UNKNOWN** (insufficient evidence). For quantitative claims, keep provenance, units, configuration and relevant dates.

## Domains
- Mission systems, spacecraft engineering and verification.
- Satellite communications, Earth observation and data services.
- Launch systems, logistics and orbital infrastructure.
- Robotics, autonomy, navigation, power, propulsion, ISRU and habitats.
- Science, higher education, domestic manufacturing, economics and regulation.

## Public research policy
External missions are studied as evidence and potential comparative cases, never as presumed partnerships. Do not treat a proposal as a deployment or a target as an achieved result. We do not disclose confidential contact histories or private IP.
