# Cosmic Programm Technical Roadmap v1.0

## Stage 0 — Knowledge and architecture
External-programme knowledge base; source register; normalized parameters; capability matrix; technology-gap map; public architecture.

## Stage 1 — Orbital demonstrator
Demonstrate spacecraft autonomy, communications, power, payload integration and orbital mission control.

Outputs: onboard computer/autonomy loop, command and telemetry architecture, payload interface, fault-management data, ground operations and qualification process.

## Stage 2 — Lunar resource scout
Demonstrate autonomous lunar sensing and acquisition of resource-relevant data.

Outputs: lunar navigation, terrain/illumination characterization, thermal resilience, communications architecture and resource-data products.

## Stage 3 — Lunar infrastructure demonstrator
Deploy a long-duration modular node integrating power, communications, computing and payload interfaces.

Outputs: long-duration surface operations, a modular utility interface, autonomous health management and surface-data relay.

## Stage 4 — Repeatable logistics
Move from demonstrations to repeatable missions: standardized interfaces, procurement, launch-vehicle integration, mission control, spare parts and repeatable qualification.

## Stage 5 — Resource-processing pilot
Experimentally validate a selected resource-processing chain. The specific technology is not fixed until sufficient Stage 2 data is obtained.

## Stage 6 — Permanent lunar utility network
Combine multiple nodes into interoperable surface infrastructure.

## Stage 7 — Preliminary Mars missions
Transfer verified autonomy, power, communications, logistics and resource-prospecting capabilities to Martian conditions.

## Programme invariant

Every stage must create a capability, dataset or interface that reduces uncertainty in the next stage.
