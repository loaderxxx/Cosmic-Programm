# Programme Economic Model Framework v1.0

## Purpose

The economic model should show not only the price of one mission, but also the cost of accumulating the next verified capability.

## Cost layers

1. research;
2. design;
3. prototyping;
4. qualification;
5. production;
6. integration;
7. launch;
8. communications and ground segment;
9. operations;
10. data analysis;
11. maintenance;
12. reuse;
13. decommissioning.

## Principal metrics

- mission cost;
- cost per kilogram of delivered equipment;
- cost per operating hour;
- cost per unit of useful data;
- cost per verified capability;
- cost of repetition;
- cost of failure;
- cost of a design change.

## Scaling

Model separately:
**single demonstrator → small series → repeatable series → infrastructure system.**

The key parameter is not the minimum initial price, but the change in cost and risk through repetition.
