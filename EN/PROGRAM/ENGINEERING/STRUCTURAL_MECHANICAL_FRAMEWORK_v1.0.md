# Structural and Mechanical System Framework v1.0

## Purpose

Define a public engineering framework for spacecraft structure, mechanical loads, vibration, payload attachment and integration.

## Load cases

Consider separately:
- transportation;
- static loads;
- vibration;
- acoustic loads;
- shock loads;
- separation;
- deployment;
- manoeuvres;
- landing, where applicable;
- thermal deformation.

## Structural model

For each element, record:
- mass;
- geometry;
- material;
- centre of mass;
- moments of inertia;
- interfaces;
- temperature range;
- allowable loads;
- factor of safety;
- verification method.

## Centre of mass and inertia

Centre of mass and moments of inertia must be known at the following stages:
1. early model;
2. engineering model;
3. assembled spacecraft;
4. pre-launch configuration.

Payload or equipment changes must be reflected in the model.

## Verification

Use analysis, static testing, vibration testing, acoustic testing and deployable-mechanism verification where applicable.
