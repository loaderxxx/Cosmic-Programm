# Mission B — Lunar Resource Scout — System Requirements v0.1

## Objective
Produce a high-value, georeferenced lunar polar dataset for terrain, illumination, thermal environment, communications conditions and resource indicators.

## Architecture
Commercial launch + commercial/partner lunar delivery where practical + autonomous payload + rover only if the science question requires mobility.

## Baseline target
- payload: 20–100 kg class
- surface mission: minimum 30 Earth days; stretch target multiple lunar daylight cycles
- precision landing: supplier-dependent; requirement derived from target site and science value
- communications: direct-to-Earth and/or relay
- autonomy: hazard-aware surface operations

ESA Argonaut demonstrates the direction of modern lunar logistics: up to 1,500 kg cargo, autonomous landing, 250 m first-flight landing accuracy and five-year surface design lifetime. Our Scout is intentionally far smaller and is a technology/data mission, not an Argonaut competitor. citeturn0search7

## Measurements
- high-resolution terrain mapping
- illumination duration
- temperature
- radiation/environmental conditions
- volatile/resource indicators
- communications visibility
- mobility/hazard data if rover is included

## Success condition
Data must change at least one engineering decision for the following infrastructure mission.

## Verification
Earth analogue tests, thermal-vacuum, navigation simulation, autonomous landing/operations simulation, payload calibration, communications end-to-end test.

## Critical risks
landing failure, thermal survival, communications blackout, dust/terrain interaction, insufficient resource signal, inadequate power.

## Gate
No infrastructure design freeze until the Scout dataset has been independently analysed and translated into requirements.
