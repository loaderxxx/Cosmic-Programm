# Cosmic Programm — Technical Presentation Outline v1.0

## 1. Why space programmes are systems
A modern exploration programme is a network of transportation, power, communications, autonomy, logistics, science and infrastructure rather than one spacecraft.

## 2. How leading programmes are built
NASA: objective -> capability -> function -> element.
SpaceX: reusable transportation -> high cadence -> logistics/refuelling.
ESA: modular launcher and payload/service architecture.
Blue Origin: reusable heavy transport + integrated manufacturing.
China: robotic precursors -> infrastructure.

## 3. The technical stack
Earth -> LEO -> cislunar -> lunar orbit -> lunar surface -> resource utilization -> infrastructure -> Mars.

## 4. Engineering numbers
Show verified examples:
- SLS core: 64.6 m long, 8.4 m diameter, four RS-25 engines.
- Falcon 9: 70 m, 22.8 t published LEO payload.
- Starship: 124 m, 9 m, 100+ t fully reusable target.
- Ariane 64: 21.6 t published LEO payload.
- New Glenn: 45 t published LEO payload.
Cite each number in the final presentation.

## 5. What the documents reveal
Interfaces and operations are as important as propulsion.
Ground infrastructure is part of the launch system.
Qualification is a major programme phase.
Data and communications are architectural resources.
Reuse changes logistics and economics.
Modularity creates supplier flexibility.

## 6. Cosmic Programm concept
Start with capabilities, not a launcher.
Mission A -> orbital autonomy.
Mission B -> lunar resource intelligence.
Mission C -> persistent lunar infrastructure.

## 7. Build vs buy
Show the boundary between commodity transportation and proprietary capability.

## 8. Technology roadmap
Autonomy -> cislunar operations -> precision landing -> power -> resource sensing -> ISRU -> infrastructure.

## 9. Proof strategy
Every major claim has a source.
Every requirement has a verification method.
Every mission leaves reusable capability.
Every planned capability is labelled separately from demonstrated capability.

## 10. Closing
Cosmic Programm is designed as an evolving technical system, not a single vehicle project.
