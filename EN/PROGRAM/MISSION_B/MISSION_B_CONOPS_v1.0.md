# Mission B — CONOPS v1.0

## Purpose

The lunar resource scout should obtain data about a selected surface region and verify autonomous operation under lunar environmental conditions.

## Phases

1. launch;
2. translunar transfer;
3. navigation;
4. landing preparation;
5. landing;
6. initial checkout;
7. mapping;
8. environmental measurement;
9. resource prospecting;
10. repeat observations;
11. data transmission;
12. completion or extension of the experiment.

## Data priorities

1. spacecraft safety;
2. geometry and position;
3. power-system status;
4. communications status;
5. environmental data;
6. resource data;
7. secondary scientific observations.

## Principle

Do not fix a particular resource-extraction or processing technology before obtaining data. First establish the resource's presence, distribution, accessibility and uncertainty.
