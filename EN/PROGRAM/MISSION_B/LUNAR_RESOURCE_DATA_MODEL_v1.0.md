# Mission B — Resource Data Model v1.0

## Purpose

Define the data needed to move from a general resource assumption to an engineering-useful decision.

## Hierarchy

1. presence;
2. spatial distribution;
3. depth;
4. concentration;
5. physical state;
6. accessibility;
7. variability;
8. quantity of accessible resource;
9. measurement uncertainty;
10. suitability for a subsequent process.

## Measurements

For every measurement, record:
- coordinates;
- time;
- method;
- resolution;
- accuracy;
- environmental conditions;
- repeatability;
- data provenance.

## Decision

Extraction design cannot proceed solely on the basis of detecting a signal. An assessment of the resource's engineering accessibility is required.
