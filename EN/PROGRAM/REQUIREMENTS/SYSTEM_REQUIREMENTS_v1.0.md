# Cosmic Programm System Requirements v1.0

Status: preliminary requirements derived from the architecture.

## SYS-001 Traceability
Every flight requirement shall be linked to a mission objective, a function and a verification method.

## SYS-002 Evidence
Every quantitative requirement shall have a source or an engineering derivation.

## SYS-003 Modular payload
The first-generation architecture shall support replaceable payload modules without redesigning the entire spacecraft.

## SYS-004 Communications
Defined command, telemetry and payload-data channels shall exist, with documented interfaces and link assumptions.

## SYS-005 Autonomous recovery
Fault detection, transition to a safe state and recovery shall be implemented for critical failures.

## SYS-006 Power
An explicit budget shall exist for generation, storage, distribution and shedding of non-essential loads.

## SYS-007 Thermal control
The environment, operating range, heat-rejection scheme and survival mode shall be defined.

## SYS-008 Attitude control and navigation
Attitude-knowledge and attitude-control accuracy, sensors, actuators and navigation sources shall be defined.

## SYS-009 Verification
Every requirement shall be verified by analysis, inspection, test, demonstration or flight results.

## SYS-010 Knowledge reuse
Every mission shall produce engineering data usable by subsequent missions.

## SYS-011 External interfaces
Launch-vehicle and ground-servicing interfaces shall be documented separately from internal spacecraft interfaces.

## SYS-012 Public evidence
Mature, non-confidential technical data shall be published with explicit identification of objectives, assumptions and unknowns.
