# ISRO — Gaganyaan / Lunar and Planetary Exploration

## Gaganyaan
Gaganyaan is India's human-spaceflight programme. Public ISRO material describes a human-rated launch vehicle, crew module, service systems, crew escape capability, life-support systems, training, qualification and a staged uncrewed test campaign before crewed flight.

## Qualification philosophy
The programme uses progressive subsystem and system-level qualification, including propulsion, crew-module structures, parachutes, separation systems and abort-related testing. ISRO reports extensive ground testing as part of human-rating work.

## Broader exploration
India's exploration portfolio also includes Chandrayaan lunar missions and Venus/planetary programmes, creating a technology base spanning launch, spacecraft, planetary science and human spaceflight.
