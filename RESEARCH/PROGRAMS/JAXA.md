# JAXA — Lunar / ISS Logistics / Deep-Space Exploration

## HTV-X
HTV-X is Japan's next-generation uncrewed cargo vehicle for low Earth orbit logistics. Its design increases cargo capability and operational flexibility and is intended to support future Gateway logistics as part of international lunar exploration.

## Engineering themes
- autonomous rendezvous and cargo logistics
- modular payload accommodation
- LEO logistics as a bridge to cislunar logistics
- international spacecraft interfaces
