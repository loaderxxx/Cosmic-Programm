# CNSA / China — Chang'e / ILRS / Tianwen

## Lunar exploration
China's lunar program combines robotic sample/technology missions with a longer-term lunar infrastructure concept. Chang'e-7 is planned for the lunar south-polar region and includes high-precision landing and exploration of permanently shadowed areas. Chang'e-8 is associated with resource-utilization demonstrations and preparation for the International Lunar Research Station (ILRS).

## ILRS
Public Chinese materials describe ILRS as a long-term international lunar research infrastructure concept covering lunar orbit and surface operations, transportation, power, communications, navigation, science and resource utilization.

## Engineering themes
- polar terrain and permanently shadowed-region exploration
- autonomous landing and surface mobility
- resource utilization demonstrations
- distributed lunar infrastructure
- integration of orbital and surface assets
