# ESA — Terrae Novae / Argonaut / Ariane 6 / Space Rider

## Terrae Novae
ESA's exploration roadmap covers low Earth orbit, the Moon and Mars, combining robotic and human exploration with international partnerships.

## Argonaut
Argonaut is Europe's lunar lander programme for autonomous cargo delivery. ESA describes landers launched on four-booster Ariane 6 vehicles from the 2030s, with up to 1,500 kg delivered to the lunar surface. The first proposed mission targets an end-to-2030 launch and south-polar landing demonstration.

## Ariane 6
Ariane 6 is Europe's modular launcher family. The four-booster configuration uses P160C solid-propellant boosters; ESA reported a June 2026 flight carrying 36 Amazon Leo satellites, demonstrating the higher-capacity configuration.

## Space Rider
Space Rider is a reusable uncrewed orbital platform intended for microgravity research, technology demonstration, payload operations and controlled re-entry/recovery.

## Engineering observations
Europe's architecture emphasizes modular launcher configurations, multinational industrial integration, autonomous lunar cargo delivery and reusable orbital research infrastructure. Argonaut also provides an independent European lunar logistics capability while supporting international exploration.
