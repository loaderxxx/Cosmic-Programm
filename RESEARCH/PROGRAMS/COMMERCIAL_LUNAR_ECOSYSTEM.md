# Commercial Lunar Ecosystem — Technical Baseline

## Core service categories
The commercial lunar ecosystem can be decomposed into:
1. launch services
2. lunar delivery / landers
3. navigation and communications
4. payload hosting
5. surface mobility
6. power
7. sample return
8. cislunar logistics
9. crewed landing systems
10. surface infrastructure

## NASA CLPS model
NASA's Commercial Lunar Payload Services initiative purchases lunar delivery services from commercial providers and uses missions to test science and technology payloads. The model links commercial transport capacity with government-funded technology demonstration and science objectives.

## Technical significance
The commercial model creates modular suppliers for the lunar architecture instead of requiring one vertically integrated government stack. This increases the importance of payload interfaces, navigation, thermal design, communications, landing accuracy, power budgets, surface operations and acceptance testing.
