# Roscosmos — Orbital Station / Launchers / Lunar Programme

## Current architecture themes
Russian public programme materials emphasize the Russian Orbital Station, automated operations, satellite constellation development, launch-cost reduction and commercialization.

## Launch systems
Angara is the current modular launch-family direction, alongside established launch infrastructure and spacecraft systems.

## Lunar engineering lesson
The Luna-25 failure remains a useful public engineering case: an incorrect/abnormal engine-burn profile led to a collision with the lunar surface, with subsequent analysis identifying control-system and measurement/command handling issues. This should be studied as a verification, fault-detection and guidance-control case rather than as a generalized judgement about the programme.
