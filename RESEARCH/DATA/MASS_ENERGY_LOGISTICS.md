# Mass / Energy / Logistics — Engineering Comparison Framework

## Mass accounting

Every vehicle or infrastructure element should eventually be decomposed into:
- dry mass
- propellant mass
- payload
- consumables
- structural fraction
- thermal protection
- avionics
- power
- communications
- landing/recovery hardware

## Energy accounting

Capture:
- source
- generated power
- stored energy
- peak load
- average load
- conversion losses
- thermal rejection
- mission duration

## Logistics

Capture:
- launch cadence
- cargo class
- transfer vehicle
- docking/interface
- storage
- replenishment
- maintenance
- spares
- return capability
- surface transport
- crew/cargo coupling

The purpose is to turn isolated specifications into end-to-end system budgets rather than a catalogue of disconnected numbers.
