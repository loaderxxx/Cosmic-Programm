# Power / ISRU — Technical Dataset

## Power

Capture:
- source: solar / nuclear / other
- peak and continuous power
- energy storage
- eclipse survival
- thermal rejection
- distribution voltage
- mass per delivered kW
- lifetime
- degradation
- redundancy

## ISRU

Capture:
- target resource
- feedstock
- extraction method
- energy input
- throughput
- product purity
- consumable requirements
- equipment mass
- thermal environment
- autonomy
- demonstrated scale
- maturity
- downstream use

NASA defines ISRU as systems that extract resources on the Moon or Mars and generate useful products. The current architecture therefore treats resource extraction as a formal capability layer.
