# NASA — Technical Documentation Index

## SLS Reference Guide
Official NASA SLS Program Office reference documentation. Artemis II guide: 82 pages; Artemis I guide: 86 pages. Covers programme overview, technical elements, testing, roles and facilities.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/space-launch-system/reference-guide/

## Artemis I Reference Guide
Detailed SLS, Orion and ground-system descriptions, technical elements, testing and facilities.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Artemis20I20Reference20Guide_Inter.pdf

## SLS Avionics
Flight computers, engine controllers, booster/core avionics, integration testing and flight-software verification. NASA describes three core-stage flight computers with two-of-three redundancy and an end-to-end Systems Integration Laboratory.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/sls-avionics/

## Gateway Capabilities
Operational concept, remote operation, design life, crew duration and international interoperability.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/gateway-capabilities/

## Human Landing Systems
Landing-system architecture, docking, landing, crew habitation, surface operations and return.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/human-landing-systems/

## HLS RF Communications ConOps
Formal communications concept covering Earth, Gateway, suits and lunar surface assets; downloadable PDF.
Source: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250003508

## Moon-to-Mars Architecture Definition Documents
Master architecture source set, including technology/data gaps.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/moontomarsarchitecture-architecturedefinitiondocuments/
