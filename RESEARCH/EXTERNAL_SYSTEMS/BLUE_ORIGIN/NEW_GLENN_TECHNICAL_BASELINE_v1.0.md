# Blue Origin New Glenn — Technical Baseline v1.0

## System role
Large reusable orbital launcher.

## Public parameters
- height: >98 m
- payload fairing: 7 m
- LEO payload: 45 t
- GTO payload: >13 t
- first stage: seven BE-4
- BE-4 thrust: 2,846 kN sea-level per engine
- second stage: two restartable BE-3U
- BE-3U thrust: 890 kN vacuum per engine
- first-stage reuse design: minimum 25 flights

## Operations
Blue Origin describes manufacturing, integration, launch, refurbishment and re-flight facilities within approximately 14 km around Cape Canaveral.

## Engineering lessons
- very large payload envelope
- reusable first stage
- integrated manufacturing/refurbishment site
- restartable upper-stage propulsion
- human-flight-oriented redundancy and safety design intent
