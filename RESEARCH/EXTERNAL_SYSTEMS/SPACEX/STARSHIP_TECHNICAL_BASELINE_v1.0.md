# SpaceX Starship — Technical Baseline v1.0

## System role
Large reusable transportation architecture intended for Earth orbit, Moon, Mars and beyond.

## Public parameters
- integrated height: 124 m
- diameter: 9 m
- fully reusable payload target: 100+ t
- Starship propellant: 1,600 t
- Starship thrust: 1,614 tf
- Super Heavy propellant: 3,650 t
- Super Heavy thrust: 8,240 tf
- Super Heavy engines: 33 Raptor
- Starship engines: 3 Raptor + 3 Raptor Vacuum

## Propulsion
Subcooled methane and liquid oxygen. Raptor is a reusable methane/oxygen staged-combustion engine.

## Payload architecture
The Starship User's Guide includes preliminary payload accommodations, mechanical interfaces, payload environments and mass-to-orbit information.

## System-level architecture
Vehicle -> launch/recovery -> payload deployment -> orbital operations -> tanker/refuelling -> high-energy mission -> lunar/Mars landing.

## Engineering lessons
The distinguishing architecture is the coupling of vehicle reuse, high payload volume, manufacturing cadence and orbital propellant transfer.

## Evidence rule
Published capability is not automatically equivalent to demonstrated operational capability; maintain both fields separately.
