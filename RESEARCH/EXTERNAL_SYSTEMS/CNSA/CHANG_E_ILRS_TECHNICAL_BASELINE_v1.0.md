# China — Chang'e / ILRS Technical Baseline v1.0

## Architecture
China's lunar programme combines robotic exploration, sample/technology missions and a longer-term International Lunar Research Station concept.

## Technical themes
- lunar polar exploration
- autonomous landing
- surface mobility
- permanently shadowed-region investigation
- resource utilization
- communications/navigation
- distributed lunar infrastructure

## ILRS
Public programme material describes a long-term lunar research infrastructure including orbital and surface elements, transportation, power, communications, navigation, science and resource utilization.

## Engineering lesson
A long sequence of robotic missions can reduce uncertainty before permanent infrastructure is deployed.

## Evidence rule
Mission schedules and programme status must be date-stamped and re-verified before presentation.
