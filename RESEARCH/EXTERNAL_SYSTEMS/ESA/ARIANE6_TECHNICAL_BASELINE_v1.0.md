# ESA Ariane 6 — Technical Baseline v1.0

## Architecture
Modular launcher with Ariane 62 and Ariane 64 configurations.

## Public performance
Ariane 62: approximately 10.3 t to LEO and 4.5 t to GTO.
Ariane 64: approximately 21.6 t to LEO and 11.5 t to GTO.

## Stages
- solid boosters: two or four
- main stage: Vulcain 2.1
- upper stage: Vinci
- fairing: approximately 5.4 m diameter

## Industrial architecture
ESA describes an industrial network involving several hundred companies in 13 European countries, led by ArianeGroup.

## Mission flexibility
Multiple orbits, large payloads and rideshare for smaller satellites.

## Engineering lessons
Modularity can be achieved at launcher configuration level without maintaining completely separate vehicles.
