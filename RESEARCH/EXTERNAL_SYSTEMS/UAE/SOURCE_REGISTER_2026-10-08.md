# UAE Space Programme — Primary Source Register
Version: 0.1 | Reviewed: 2026-10-08 | Language: English | Disclosure: PUBLIC RESEARCH
Scope: UAE national space ecosystem. Links identify primary publication, NOT automatic independent validation of self-reported performance.

## Evidence conventions
- FACT: directly verifiable dated event, legal text or official organisational responsibility.
- SOURCE CLAIM: published statement/target/performance assertion from an institution or supplier; not independently proven.
- INTERPRETATION: reasoned systems-level synthesis; never attribute to the source.
- ESTIMATE: explicit calculated assumption, units and method required.
- HYPOTHESIS: proposition requiring a falsification test.
- UNKNOWN: evidence not found or not yet examined.
- Maturity flags: OPERATING / COMPLETED / DEVELOPMENT / PROPOSED / REPLANNED / UNVERIFIED.
- Exact technology data must preserve configuration, revision, units and date. A planned milestone is not an achieved milestone.

## Source conflict alerts
1. **Strategy 2030 vs 2031:** the 2019 framework is real, but the Agency's April/May 2026 releases explicitly cite Strategy 2031 [S02,S03,S04]. Full 2031 primary document remains OPEN.
2. **Gateway:** 2024 airlock agreement [S23] is historical, while NASA's March 2026 pivot [S24] supersedes former deployment assumptions. Stale Gateway web pages may remain accessible.
3. **SAR mission namespace:** MBRSC Etihad-SAT [S20], UAE Space Agency Sirb [S26], Space42 Foresight [S27] are separate tracks unless a primary source demonstrates a specific organisational relationship.
4. **Performance and spend:** public commitments, mission targets, corporate resolution/benefit figures, and fund sizes must not be reported as independently achieved metrics.

For every change: record source owner, publication date, last checked date, claim type, maturity and previous version. Review critical developments monthly; regulatory notices and major mission pivots sooner. Research only; no agency endorsement or affiliation.
