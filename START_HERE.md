## Understand COSMOSYNTH in one visit

We are building a research foundation for a long-term space programme. Our work connects institutions and companies to technologies, capabilities, interfaces and mission questions. The value we seek is a defensible next engineering decision, not a large collection of unqualified claims.

| You are visiting as… | Suggested reading route | A useful next conversation |
|---|---|---|
| A space agency or sector-development organization | [UAE entry](ATLAS/REGIONS/UAE/README.md) → [Atlas](ATLAS/README.md) → [collaboration brief](COLLABORATE.md) | Which concrete research question and organizational route are appropriate? |
| A space company or technology provider | [Organization directory](ATLAS/ORGANIZATIONS/README.md) → [capabilities](ATLAS/CAPABILITIES/README.md) → [relationships](ATLAS/RELATIONSHIPS/README.md) | Is the profile accurate, and which non-confidential interface merits study? |

## What exists, and what does not follow from it

The repository contains programme studies and conceptual engineering documents. The linked public industry working set contains 55 source-graded organization/brand dossiers and eight illustrative system configurations. Some identities and many technical interfaces remain unresolved. [Read the coverage record](RESEARCH_STATUS.md).

A company entry does not establish a relationship with COSMOSYNTH. A product source does not establish qualification. A concept does not establish a working integration. A translated page does not independently validate its sources.

## Read one complete evidence trail

Choose an organization; open its capability and source documents; inspect the relevant system option; identify one missing interface or operating constraint; then read the proposed test. An unanswered question is a valid research result when the next verification step is explicit.

Private correspondence and contact histories are not available in this public repository. Public corrections and non-confidential questions can be raised through the [repository Issues](https://github.com/loaderxxx/Cosmic-Programm/issues). Do not post confidential material there.
