**Status: proposed research and engineering concepts. No completed pilot is established by this page.** The purpose is to make the next proof smaller and clearer, not to rebrand a concept as a deployed capability.

## Mission A — operations and autonomy

The existing concept concerns orbital autonomy and logistics. A suitable first research step may be an offline ground-contact and recovery scenario: synthetic inputs, explicit resource ownership, a reference scheduling rule, one fault and a reproducible event log. Acceptance criteria and independent review must be agreed before calling it a partner pilot.

## Mission B — lunar resource intelligence

The concept studies what environmental/resource data would be needed for later engineering decisions. A paper study or data exercise is not evidence of a lunar payload, landing capability or accessible resource.

[Mission B concept](../PROGRAM/MISSION_B/MISSION_B_CONOPS_v1.0.md) · [Resource-data model](../PROGRAM/MISSION_B/LUNAR_RESOURCE_DATA_MODEL_v1.0.md).

## Mission C — modular infrastructure

The concept examines power, communications, payload hosting and growth of an infrastructure node. It depends on requirements, interfaces, resources and verified enabling capabilities; it is not an announced flight programme.

[Mission C concept](../PROGRAM/MISSION_C/MISSION_C_CONOPS_v1.0.md) · [Service model](../PROGRAM/MISSION_C/INFRASTRUCTURE_SERVICE_MODEL_v1.0.md).

## Minimum pilot agreement

Name the problem owner, test question, permitted inputs, reference baseline, measured output, pass/fail rule, reviewer, resource limit and rights to results. Explicitly exclude flight control, RF transmission, paid services, procurement and external commitments unless separately authorized.
