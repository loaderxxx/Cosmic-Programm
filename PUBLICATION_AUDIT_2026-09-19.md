## Important interpretation

Cosmic Programm is **not a NASA/ESA program, company, institutional partnership or funded space mission**.

It is an independent, public systems-research project that uses public sources as inputs and develops its own hypotheses, architecture and experiments.

## Recommended public wording

Do not claim:
- that the program is already an operational space program;
- that a specific mission is funded;
- that NASA/ESA endorse it;
- that a particular technology is proven when it is only a hypothesis;
- that publication establishes patent rights or legal protection.
