## Who we are

COSMOSYNTH is an independent space-systems research programme. We connect published evidence, organizations, capabilities and interfaces to mission questions and proposed tests. Our current maturity is research and conceptual engineering, not demonstrated flight capability.

## Why discuss a question with your organization?

An agency can help clarify programme relevance and the correct institutional route. A company can challenge a capability description or explain a public interface. A university or engineering team can help define a reproducible question and review the evidence. These are invitations, not existing arrangements.

## Problem and proposed value

Space-system decisions often require information from several technical and organizational layers. Our proposed contribution is a bounded source review, capability/interface comparison or reproducible study with explicit assumptions and uncertainties. We do not promise universal expertise, a validated speed advantage or a result before scope is agreed.

## One possible pilot

Agree on one non-confidential operations or data question. Define a reference approach and one failure or edge case. Produce a traceable input set, a reproducible result, a comparison and a limitations note. A ground-contact scheduling/recovery study is one candidate; it is not a preselected product or a completed integration.

## Required input

A problem owner or technical reviewer; a narrow question; public or explicitly permitted data/interfaces; a success criterion; and agreed scope, available resources and rights to outputs. Budget, timeline, data access and institutional eligibility are not presumed.

## Next step

Select the question and the person/team qualified to review it. For a public correction or non-confidential introduction, use the [repository Issues](https://github.com/loaderxxx/Cosmic-Programm/issues). For confidential discussion, first agree on an authorized private channel; do not post personal data, contracts, credentials or unpublished technical material in a public issue.

No agency logo, company inclusion or source citation in this repository represents endorsement. Publication, external messaging, funding applications, procurement and deployment remain separate approval steps.
